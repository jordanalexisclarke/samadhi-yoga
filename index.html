01 / THE PRACTICE

Your mat.
Your expression.

Vinyasa means “to place with intention.” Find your starting point, connect to your breath and let the practice unfold.

01 — LEARN

Vinyasa 101

BEGINNERS / ALL LEVELS

An approachable introduction to Vinyasa, breaking down foundational poses, breathwork and transitions at a slower pace. Perfect for beginners or anyone looking for a grounding reset.

02 — FLOW

Hot Vinyasa Flow

ALL LEVELS

An energizing Vinyasa flow that connects breath to movement while building strength, balance and mobility. Designed for anyone looking to move with intention and deepen their practice.

03 — EXPLORE

Strong Vinyasa Flow

INTERMEDIATE

Take the foundations further with challenging variations, creative transitions and opportunities to explore your edge. The goal isn’t the perfect pose, but discovering your fullest expression of it.

02 / MEET YOUR TEACHER

Hi, I’m Jordan.
Let’s move together.

I’m Jordan Clarke, a certified Vinyasa Yoga teacher with a dynamic approach to movement. My classes are fueled by breath to movement, presence and fluidity.

Technique, form, mobility and strength training are at the core of my classes, alongside introspection and presence. I encourage you to challenge yourself while making space to meet you where you’re at. My aim is for you to leave feeling stronger, more grounded and relaxed.

Music is part of the experience too — from roots rock reggae to ballads, classic zen or house. Each class is curated around the students, their needs, their energy and their vibe.
